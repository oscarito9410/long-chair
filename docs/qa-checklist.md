# QA checklist

For the iOS simulator. The web demo at `#/` mirrors the same steps. English copy only. No location prompt.

## Welcome

- [ ] One primary action starts onboarding.
- [ ] "Use a sample kit" is a text link and opens the kit.
- [ ] The settings gear is at the top.
- [ ] The sample and "Not medical advice" lines are visible.

## What do you love?

- [ ] The step is labeled 1 of 4. The chair stays the next step.
- [ ] A Region chip sits above the groups. It names the locale region, or Global.
- [ ] Mexico shows Liga MX, telenovelas, and Bad Bunny, plus one or two chips from outside Mexico.
- [ ] United States shows NFL, sitcoms, and the other US chips.
- [ ] The region menu lists names, not flag emoji. The choice survives a reload.
- [ ] Search still replaces the groups. A typed title can be added.
- [ ] Three picks are required. The button explains what is missing until then.
- [ ] "Use a sample kit instead" resets to the sample kit.

## How long, themes, company

- [ ] The dial offers 1h, 2h, 3h, and 4h.
- [ ] Illness, Hospitals, Cancer, Losing a parent, and Grief start on.
- [ ] Any theme can be turned off.
- [ ] A custom theme can be added and is labeled keyword-only, lower confidence.
- [ ] Company can be chosen or skipped.

## Building and kit

- [ ] The build announces progress and can skip to the kit.
- [ ] The kit lists pieces with times that add up to the chair, or shows the open minutes.
- [ ] A piece opens a sheet. The clock has a duration note: runtime, episode length, album length, or "Estimated from page count".
- [ ] The sheet has an "Open in …" button. It leaves the app. Nothing plays inside it.
- [ ] Movies and shows list a sample watch note and the JustWatch credit.
- [ ] "Why it passed" names the sample source and says it is not a live catalog check.

## Companion

- [ ] Each place shows a walk time.
- [ ] No closing time appears on the sample places.
- [ ] "Open in Maps" leaves the app.
- [ ] The places note says they are fictional.

## Compare and settings

- [ ] Compare shows the sample precision and recall and says it is not a live score.
- [ ] Settings includes the TMDB sentence and the JustWatch credit.
- [ ] Settings can open the sample kit again.
- [ ] No account, no GPS permission, no medical claim.
